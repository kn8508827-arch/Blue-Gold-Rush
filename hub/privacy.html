Cooler Bot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026 · Operator: Vid Cooler (Team ស្មោះស្នេហ៍), Cambodia · Contact: yescooler34@gmail.com

What Cooler Bot is

Cooler Bot is an AI assistant that answers messages in Khmer on Telegram (@TeamSmosSneabot) and in Facebook Messenger on the Pages "Vid Cooler" and "Vid Cooler Page Office".

Data we receive

  • The messages you send to the bot (text) and your Messenger page-scoped ID or Telegram user ID.
  • Your public name if the platform provides it.
  • For Facebook Pages connected by their admins: public posts of those Pages (to show partner news in the Cooler Hub).

How we use it

  • To reply to you. Message text is sent to Google Gemini to generate the reply.
  • A short recent chat history is kept so the bot can follow the conversation.
  • We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Retention

Chat history is kept only as long as needed to run the service. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Delete your data

See Data deletion instructions, or email yescooler34@gmail.com.
